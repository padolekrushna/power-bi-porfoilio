About

I'm Krushna Padole — 2.5+ years across BI development and AI/ML engineering, working with multiple enterprises on the same underlying problem: turning raw, scattered data into something a team can actually act on.

On the AI/ML side that means RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, and predictive modeling. On the BI side it means the same discipline applied to reporting — a clean data model and sensible DAX measures before a single chart gets styled. That overlap is usually what makes a dashboard hold up once real data hits it, not just the demo.
